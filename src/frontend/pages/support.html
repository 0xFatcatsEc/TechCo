We accept GCash, Maya, bank transfers (BDO, BPI, UnionBank), credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard), and Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders within Metro Manila.

Metro Manila orders are delivered within 1-3 business days. Provincial orders take 3-7 business days depending on location. Express shipping is available for an additional fee.

We offer a 7-day return policy for defective or incorrect items. Products must be in original packaging and unused condition. Contact our support team to initiate a return.
